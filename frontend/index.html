          How to play

          1. Two teams draft ten players each: two point guards, two shooting guards, two small forwards, two power forwards and two centers.
          2. Every round names a position and a criterion: a team, a country of birth, a decade or a college. Both teams draft a player who fits both, and nobody can be drafted twice.
          3. The order swings every round: A then B, B then A, A then B... The team that picks second in one round picks first in the next.
          4. Every draft is scored on one stat, dealt at random when it starts: height, weight, age in last season, or a career total such as games, points, rebounds or assists (NBA drafts can also deal minutes, steals and blocks). The higher team total wins.
          5. Career totals count the NBA's regular seasons, or the EuroLeague's games since 2000-01. Only players with a number in the dealt stat can be drafted.
          6. The numbers show either after the last pick, revealed one by one on the board, or as you go, as soon as each player is drafted. The scoreboard adds up each team's total either way, and the winner is only called once every number is on the board.
          7. An expired shot clock is a turnover: you get the player with the lowest number still left for that round. In Extreme search, a player who doesn't fit, is already taken or has no number in the stat costs the same.
          8. When a round is dealt, press Draft a player to search for your pick.
          9. Positions come from the player's career (a combo guard fits PG and SG). EuroLeague registrations only say guard, forward or center, so a EuroLeague guard fits both guard slots.
